The gap we target

It’s not enough to log a decision.
Can you prove what went missing?

Ordinary logs can be edited, hidden, or lost. Ghostless explores a verifiable trail for decisions that may trigger real-world effects.

THREAT / 01

Ghost actions

An agent attempts to trigger an action without a valid signed receipt.

RECEIPT-GATED ACTUATION
THREAT / 02

Silent omission

A committed decision or its evidence disappears from the operator’s history.

INCLUSION CHALLENGES
THREAT / 03

Conflicting histories

An operator signs different decisions for the same sequence position or records a false policy outcome.

How it fits together

Evidence across the decision lifecycle.

The project combines signed decision receipts, ordered windows, checkpoints, challenges, and a contract gate for downstream actions.

STEP / 01

Register & bond

Operators register and put a bond at risk under the system’s enforcement rules.

STEP / 02

Reserve sequence

Monotonic sequence slots help bound the order and timing of decisions.

STEP / 03

Seal & challenge

Window checkpoints and proof challenges provide a path to dispute omissions or conflicting claims.

STEP / 04

Gate the effect

The actuator contract checks for a qualifying receipt before allowing a protected action.
