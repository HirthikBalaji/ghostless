\begin{tcolorbox}[colframe=secondary, title=\textbf{Executive Summary}]
Ghostless is an open protocol and smart contract deployment that makes \textbf{ledger omission provable, bonded, and economically irrational}. Conventional blockchains prove that committed data was not modified; they cannot prove that an operator didn't quietly delete or omit a decision before publishing. Ghostless solves this via \textbf{pre-reserved sequence ranges}, \textbf{bonded challenge deadlines}, an \textbf{exposure-capped on-chain actuator gate}, and \textbf{victim-compensated slashing} (60\% to victim, 20\% to reporter, 20\% burned).
\end{tcolorbox}

\section{1. Explaining Ghostless to an 8th Grader}

Imagine you have a school principal who keeps a magic notebook. 
Whenever something is written in this notebook with special permanent ink, it can \textbf{never be erased or changed}. That is what traditional blockchains do—they provide \textbf{Integrity}.

\subsection{The Big Cheat: The Invisible Omission}
Now, imagine the principal promises: \textit{``If you behave well, you get a candy bar. If you get into trouble, I will write your detention in the magic notebook.''}

What happens if the principal has a secret favorite student who breaks a window? 
The principal doesn't erase anything from the notebook. Instead, the principal simply \textbf{never writes the detention down in the first place!} 
If parents look at the notebook, it looks 100\% clean. The principal didn't tamper with the records; they just \textbf{left it out}. That is called a \textbf{Ghost Decision} (Omission).

Traditional blockchains have no idea the broken window ever happened.

\subsection{How Ghostless Catches the Cheater}
Ghostless stops this cheat using four clever rules:

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Pre-Numbered Pages in Public (Reservation):}\\
    Before the principal makes any decisions today, they must announce on the public school bulletin board: \textit{``Today, exactly slots \#1 through \#64 are open.''} The numbers are fixed in advance. You can't secretly skip page 5, because everyone will see page 5 is missing.
    \item \textbf{No Ticket, No Ride (The Actuator Gate):}\\
    Whenever an action happens in the real world (e.g., dispensing cash from an ATM, unlocking a door, or releasing an escrow payment), the computer or machine refuses to move an inch unless the student holds a \textbf{digitally signed receipt} from the principal with that exact slot number on it. If the principal wants to take an action, they \textbf{must} give you a signed receipt.
    \item \textbf{The Exposure Cap (Capping the Temptation):}\\
    The escrow gate refuses to release more than $2\times$ the principal's deposited bond per window. The principal can never steal more money than they have locked up as collateral!
    \item \textbf{Victim-First Cash Penalty Trap (Bonded Slashing):}\\
    The principal deposits a large bond of MSTC tokens. If you hold a receipt for slot \#12, but the principal tries to hide it or pretend slot \#12 was blank, you wave your receipt on-chain. If the principal fails to respond within 60 seconds, the smart contract slashes their bond: \textbf{60\% goes straight to the victim who was harmed, 20\% to the reporter who caught the cheat, and 20\% is burned forever.}
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{The Honest Boundary (What We Cannot Prove)}
If the principal hands cash to someone under the table in a dark alley without using any computer or machine that asks for a ticket, no computer in the universe can prove it happened. Ghostless does not pretend to solve magic. Instead, it ensures that \textbf{every system that enforces the ticket gate can never silently drop a decision without getting caught and slashed.}

\noindent \textbf{Understanding the Gas Dynamics:}
\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{Why $N=64$ costs more than $N=128$:} In the EVM, the first transaction initializing cold storage slots incurs the 20,000 gas \texttt{SSTORE} penalty. Subsequent window transitions modify warm storage slots, reducing baseline write gas.

Tree leaves are verified off-chain and only materialized during challenges.

\section{5. Brutally Honest Prior Art \& Novelty Assessment (No Sugar-Coating)}

A literature sweep across transparency logs (RFC 6962 / RFC 9162), rollups, and verifiable data structures reveals the following precise boundary:

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=1.5em]
    \item \textbf{What Was Already Known (Prior Art)}:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{Certificate Transparency (RFC 6962)}: Signed Certificate Timestamps (SCTs) and Maximum Merge Delay (MMD) introduced signed receipts and seal deadlines. However, CT is \textbf{passive}: if a log omits an entry, there is no on-chain smart contract, no locked bond, and no programmatic slashing.
        \item \textit{Optimistic Rollups (Arbitrum/Optimism)}: Forced inclusion queues on L1 obligate an L2 sequencer to include transactions within a challenge window. Ghostless adapts this pattern to off-chain enterprise decision ledgers.
        \item \textit{Positional Merkle Trees}: Crosby--Wallach (2009) and Merkle (1979) established non-commutative fixed-index trees.
        \item \textit{Proof-of-Stake Slashing}: Double-signing penalties (Casper / Tendermint) pioneered cryptoeconomic deterrence.
    \end{itemize}
    \item \textbf{What is Genuinely Novel in Ghostless}:
    To our knowledge, no deployed protocol has combined: \textbf{(1) on-chain sequence reservation before content exists} (fixing history length in public), \textbf{(2) pull-based on-chain inclusion challenges with bonded slashing}, \textbf{(3) exposure-capped escrow gates ($k=2$)}, and \textbf{(4) dual-layer actuator gating} (off-chain API gate + on-chain smart contract escrow via \texttt{ReceiptGatedEscrow.sol}).
    \item \textbf{Fundamental Theoretical Limits}:
    \begin{itemize}
        \item \textit{The Impossibility Boundary}: If a decision leaves zero trace to any party demanding a receipt (e.g. out-of-band collusion), no protocol can prove it. Ghostless strictly bounds omissions to decisions passing through the Actuator Gate.
        \item \textit{Economic Rationality}: Slashing only deters if $P(\text{detect}) \cdot B \cdot \text{slashBps} > m \cdot g$. The exposure cap ensures $m \cdot g \le B \times k$, capping the maximum potential gain.
    \end{itemize}
\end{itemize}
